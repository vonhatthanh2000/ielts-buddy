# AI English Buddy

Welcome to **AI English Buddy**, your personal AI companion designed to help you learn English efficiently and naturally! Whether you’re aiming for IELTS 7.0 or just want to enhance your language skills, this app supports your journey.

## Features

### 1. Vocabulary Enhancement

- **Usage Examples**: Get contextual examples of how to use new vocabulary effectively.
- **Review Reminders**: The app reminds you to revisit your vocabulary, ensuring retention and mastery.

### 2. Real-Life Sentence Correction

- **Instant Feedback**: Whenever you think of a sentence, input it into the app to receive immediate corrections and suggestions for improvement.

### 3. Paragraph Grading

- **Comprehensive Feedback**: Write a full paragraph, and the AI will grade it, highlight mistakes, and provide recommendations for fixing them.

## Goal

Our main aim is to help you use English naturally and confidently, targeting an IELTS score of 7.0+.
